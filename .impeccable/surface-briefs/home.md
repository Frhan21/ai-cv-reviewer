# Home and review flow

Mode: Persuade on `/`; Operate on `/analyze` and `/results`.

Audience: Job seekers preparing an application. The primary action is to upload a CV and supply target-role context. The flow must preserve the current analysis behavior, palette, logo, and current marketing proof at the user's request.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Application Orbit turns role matching into a spatial system around the CV. It refuses the centered SaaS headline, gradient blob, feature-card grid, and decorative AI chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Pale-blue application space, navy work surfaces, blue primary signals, and violet secondary signals. Flat square-edged controls and document-like planes carry the interface. The orbit is functional: role, evidence, keywords, and feedback relate to one document.

STORY: A visitor sees the CV under review, understands that role context changes the outcome, starts the upload, waits through a legible analysis state, and receives prioritized findings rather than decorative metrics.
